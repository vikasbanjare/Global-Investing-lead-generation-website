Investing flow

INR → USD
  1. Initiate LRS remittance in your Indian bank
  2. Bank verifies and converts
  3. USD routed to GIFT City
  4. USD wallet updated, buying power added
  5. Buy US stocks or ETFs

Withdrawal flow

USD → INR
  1. Place a withdrawal request (minimum USD 20)
  2. Request enters the bank’s review process
  3. Bank converts USD to INR and credits your Indian savings account
  4. Funds credited within 1–2 working days

Zero withdrawal charges for resident Indians. USD 10 for NRIs.
